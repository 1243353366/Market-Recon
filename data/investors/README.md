# Investor research collection

A curated 50-investor index for educational study. It records strategy descriptions and starting links, not endorsements, performance rankings, or recommendations. Some entries point to secondary biographies; the `research_note` fields encourage verification with original material.

2. Howard Marks — Oaktree memos: https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memos
3. Ray Dalio — Principles: https://www.principles.com/

10. SEC EDGAR filings for reporting managers and funds, including 13F-HR: https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/

## Important interpretation notes

- A 13F is a delayed, periodic snapshot of reportable long positions—not a real-time portfolio, complete portfolio, or proof of current conviction. A filing may reflect positions as of a past quarter-end.
- 13F is not a record of insider transactions. Use Forms 3/4/5 for reportable insider transactions and verify transaction codes, ownership type, and footnotes.
- Managers may operate multiple entities, use different reporting names, or not have a reportable 13F. Resolve filer identity from SEC records rather than matching names loosely.
- Shareholder letters, interviews, books, and filings have different dates, authorship, and licensing. Respect copyright, terms of service, rate limits, and redistribution restrictions; retain public URLs and metadata, and do not republish copyrighted full text without permission.
- A historical investor's approach may be unsuitable for others and should not be presented as a promise of performance.
